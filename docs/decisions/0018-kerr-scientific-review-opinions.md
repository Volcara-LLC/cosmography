# ADR 0018: Receipt-Bound Kerr Scientific Review Opinions

**Status:** Accepted for package-only scientific-review-opinion foundation | **Date:** 2026-07-19

## Context

ADR 0017 issues one exact process-local Kerr numeric-comparison capability. It preserves the receipt-bound runner numerics, same-project reference results, provisional tolerance diagnostics, disagreements, unresolved rows, and camera-screen limitations. That record still says the external execution, executable lineage, implementation independence, mapping correctness, verifier authority, and comparison time are not authenticated by the package.

The next boundary must let two scientific specialists sign what they reviewed without laundering those missing prerequisites into independent validation. A reviewer opinion cannot prove that a synthetic fixture came from a real external solver, repair unverified source-to-executable lineage, authenticate earlier injected verifiers, create an authoritative clock, or make a same-project reference independent.

## Decision

- The package issues exactly one deterministic review subject/assignment from the exact process-issued numeric-comparison object after full private-context integrity replay. A structural copy or a legacy comparison cannot enter the boundary. Subject issuance locks that comparison to one canonical pair of reviewer identity/grant/key assignments before any opinion exists; it does not yet perform the later verifier-facing record attempt.
- The subject binds the complete comparison by ID, checksum, canonical byte length and digest; preparation, admission, plan, mapping-review, candidate, scope, summary, provisional tolerances, and ordered row identities/checksums; a domain-separated ordered digest coupling each row identity/checksum to its disposition/reasons; all observed reason codes; and the declared runner, three selection reviewers, and mapping reviewer. It repeats the upstream unauthenticated-evidence limitations and grants no authority.
- Exactly two signed opinions are required in canonical order: `relativistic-physics` and `numerical-methods`. Reviewer IDs, role-grant IDs, and key IDs must be pairwise distinct from each other and from the receipt-declared runner, all three selection reviewers, and the mapping reviewer. The exact assignments are part of the issued subject. Each subject can issue only one statement for each assigned role, and each exact statement can attempt only one detached-signature decision, preventing callers from discarding an adverse statement and regenerating a favorable one in the same process. The assignments are caller-declared. Both subject and final record say that the package does not authenticate assignment authority, reviewer qualifications, conflict clearance, credential validity, or revocation. Identity-value separation does not establish implementation independence.
- Each package-built statement binds the exact subject and comparison digests, subject freeze time, role and declared identity, bounded validity window, machine-derived numeric agreement, canonical observed reason codes, one bounded opinion, evidence digests, and mandatory limitation acknowledgements. Detached signature envelopes are capped at 64 KiB and bound by exact length and SHA-256. Evidence inputs are fixed digests rather than unbounded review prose.
- Opinions are `supports-provisional-bounded-vacuum-metric-agreement`, `does-not-support-provisional-bounded-vacuum-metric-agreement`, or `insufficient-evidence`. A supporting opinion is structurally forbidden unless the immutable machine result is `within-pre-registered-tolerance` with no disagreement, incompatibility, unresolved result, or unadjudicated camera-screen reason. The name deliberately limits support to the provisional numeric metric rather than science, implementation independence, or renderer fitness. A reviewer may oppose or defer an otherwise matching result. Negative and inconclusive opinions produce terminal records rather than exceptions, preventing retry-until-support behavior.
- Final recording accepts the exact issued comparison, its exact subject, two canonical decisions, and the two exact signature envelopes. Both envelopes are detached before decision traversal. All capability, subject, decision, chronology, role-order, support-eligibility, and identity-separation checks finish before a second attempt check placed immediately beside the `WeakSet` burn. The comparison is burned before either asynchronous injected-verifier callback. Verifier throw, rejection, malformed receipt, or binding mismatch is permanent.
- Each injected verifier receipt must bind the exact comparison, subject, statement, decision, signature bytes, reviewer role/identity values, and declared check time, and must assert current grant, key, purpose, revocation, evidence-digest, row/summary, limitation, and signature checks. Those are accepted injected-boundary assertions; `verifierAuthorityAuthenticatedByPackage` and package-authenticated scientific authority remain false.
- After both callbacks, the package repeats full comparison/private-context integrity, decision/signature integrity, and identity separation before issuing a deeply frozen process-local opinion record. The record retains each individual opinion and declared decision window, a deterministic aggregate opinion, exact ordered row IDs/checksums/dispositions/reasons plus their domain-separated binding, the full comparison summary and tolerances, and exact bindings back to all retained numeric metrics. Structural replay checks comparison-to-subject-to-decision-to-record chronology. It does not duplicate the multi-megabyte comparison and is capped at 2 MiB.
- `frozenAt`, decision times, and `recordedAt` are caller-declared canonical timestamps. Their causal ordering and a seven-day maximum decision lifetime are checked, but no authoritative clock is claimed. Scientific review happens after the completed comparison, so it is not incorrectly constrained by the expired execution-claim or mapping-review windows that governed earlier completed actions.
- The resulting state is `receipt-bound-kerr-scientific-review-opinions-recorded-insufficient-authenticated-evidence`. It records completion of this local protocol only. The issued capability is explicitly a non-authoritative prerequisite input, and the record says that authenticating it alone cannot establish validation. It enumerates the still-required real-execution, source/executable/normalizer-lineage, mapping, independence, reviewer-authority, authoritative-time/revocation, and durable-evidence prerequisites. Real execution, provenance, license, mapping correctness, implementation independence, scientific validation, independent validation, rendering, production use, placeholder replacement, release, publication, and activation all remain false. A future authenticated validation gate is intentionally separate.
- The package still performs no filesystem or network I/O, acquisition, build, shell, process, worker, native loading, sandbox operation, signing, persistence, external execution, rendering, publication, or deployment. Tests use synthetic in-memory capabilities and injected verifiers only.

## Consequences

Cosmography can now retain two exact, signed, role-separated scientific opinions over one receipt-bound comparison without allowing a positive opinion to erase machine disagreement or grant downstream authority. Failed verifier attempts cannot be replayed, and negative evidence remains a first-class terminal record.

This is not independent scientific validation. A later gate still needs authentic capabilities for real isolated execution, source-to-executable and normalizer lineage, implementation independence, mapping correctness, qualified reviewer identity and grants, authoritative time/revocation, immutable evidence retention, and a durable cross-process ledger. Renderer parity, images, emission and radiative transfer, named-device performance, accessibility, release governance, and replacement of the procedural Black-hole Lab display remain separate gates. Nothing here completes BH1, a roadmap stage, or the product.

## Validation

Acceptance requires focused tests for deterministic subject binding, both fixed roles, supporting/opposing/inconclusive opinions, support rejection over disagreed or unadjudicated comparisons, exact signature and verifier-receipt binding, every prior-role and pairwise identity collision, chronology boundaries, structural copies, accessor and Proxy reentrancy, asynchronous concurrency, permanent burn after verifier failure, negative-opinion retention, self versus exact issued integrity, deep freezing, max-grid byte headroom, static no-I/O imports, and literal-false validation/render/release authority.

Those tests validate only the bounded local protocol. They cannot establish that any current fixture is a real independent implementation, that any injected verifier is authentic, or that any opinion is sufficient for scientific validation or renderer use.

At this checkpoint, the focused receipt/review suite passes 40 tests and the complete relativity package passes typecheck plus 120 tests. The repository-wide scientific-model, connector, manifest, web lint, production-build, and web test matrix also remains green; these are software-contract checks over synthetic or frozen inputs, not scientific validation.
